## What the evidence does and does not prove

- `frame-proof.json` and the PNG thumbnails prove that the **recompiled native binary** emitted content with more than one colour on this host. They are **not** a Windows oracle.
- `highball.log` records attempts to launch the original `.exe` under Wine/Highball. Timeouts and missing DLLs are negative results; they do not establish equivalence.
- A B1 `BUILD-ONLY` status stays BUILD-ONLY even if a later test run launched the binary: the run/verify stages of the pipeline were not executed.
- No game binary, generated project tree or full-size frame is vendored into this repository; only small logs, JSON reports and downscaled PNG previews are copied.
